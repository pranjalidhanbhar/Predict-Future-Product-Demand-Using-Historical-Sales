# Predict-Future-Product-Demand-Using-Historical-Sales
The project “Predict Future Product Demand Using Historical Sales” focuses on analyzing past sales data to forecast future product demand using data analytics and machine learning techniques. The system leverages historical sales patterns, pricing, discounts, seasonal trends, and customer behavior to generate accurate demand predictions.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing historical sales data to understand product demand patterns and generate insights for future demand forecasting. The project combines data analytics, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and machine learning techniques to support data-driven business decisions.

The analysis helps identify important patterns related to sales, pricing, discounts, seasonal trends, and customer behavior. The project also includes an interactive dashboard to present key findings and business insights in a clear and user-friendly manner.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Accurate product demand forecasting is important for businesses to maintain appropriate inventory levels, reduce operational costs, avoid stock shortages, and improve overall business planning.

Historical sales data contains valuable information about customer demand and purchasing patterns. However, analyzing large amounts of sales data manually can make it difficult to identify trends and make reliable decisions.

This project aims to analyze historical sales information and use data-driven techniques to understand demand patterns and support future demand forecasting.
